# Expert code of conduct
## What experts must not do
Experts must not ask customers for personal contacts, social media accounts or off-platform payments. Experts must not give medical, legal or financial instructions, and must not tell customers to end relationships or make major life decisions.

## Reporting misconduct
Complaints about expert behaviour are escalated to the Expert Quality team, which reviews the chat transcript within 48 hours. The agent must not promise a specific outcome (such as the expert being banned) before the review.
