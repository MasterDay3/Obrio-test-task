# Cancel a subscription bought on the website
## Steps
Open the Nebula website, sign in, go to Profile → Manage subscription and press Cancel subscription. The customer receives a confirmation email within a few minutes.

## What happens after cancelling
Access stays active until the end of the paid period. No further renewals are charged. Cancelling does not refund the current period automatically; see the refund policy.

## Cancelling on behalf of the customer
If the customer cannot access the website, an agent can cancel the web subscription from the admin panel after verifying the account email.
