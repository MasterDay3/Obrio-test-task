# Free trial terms
## How the trial works
New customers can start a 7-day trial. The trial is free; the card is authorised but not charged. On day 8 the selected plan is charged automatically unless the trial was cancelled before it ended.

## Reminders
A reminder email is sent 24 hours before the trial ends.

## Forgot to cancel the trial
If the first charge happened less than 7 days ago and the app was not used after the charge, the customer is eligible for a refund under the refund policy.
