# Refund policy
## Who is eligible
Web purchases (made at nebula website checkout) can be refunded within 14 days of the charge if the paid feature was not substantially used. Subscription renewals can be refunded within 7 days of the renewal date if the customer did not use the app after the renewal.

## App Store and Google Play purchases
Purchases made through the Apple App Store or Google Play are processed by Apple or Google. Nebula cannot issue these refunds directly. The customer must request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (iOS) or through the Google Play order history (Android).

## Amounts and approvals
Support agents may approve refunds up to $100 without escalation. Refunds above $100, refunds outside the 14-day window, and repeated refund requests from the same account must be approved by a Billing specialist.

## How refunds are paid
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks usually show the money within 5–10 business days. Refunds cannot be sent to a different card or as cash.
