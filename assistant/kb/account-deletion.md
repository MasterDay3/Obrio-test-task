# Account deletion and personal data requests (GDPR)
## Deletion requests
Customers can delete their account in Profile → Settings → Delete account. Requests made to support are forwarded to the Privacy team, which completes them within 30 days as required by GDPR.

## Data access requests
Requests for a copy of personal data or billing records are handled only by the Privacy or Legal team. Agents must not send data exports themselves.

## Active subscriptions
Deleting an account does not cancel App Store or Google Play subscriptions; the customer must cancel them in the store first.
