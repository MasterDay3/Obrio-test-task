# Readings disclaimer and crisis support
## Entertainment purposes
Readings and horoscopes are for entertainment and self-reflection. They are not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice.

## Customers in crisis
If a customer mentions self-harm or suicide, the agent must not use a template. Escalate to Trust & Safety immediately and share local crisis resources, such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the US or local emergency services.
