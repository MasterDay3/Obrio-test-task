# Cannot log in / magic link not arriving
## Magic link email
Check spam and promotions folders. Links expire after 15 minutes and only the latest link works. Corporate mail filters sometimes block the email.

## Session expired after update
After an app update, log out, force-close the app and sign in again. If the error persists, reinstall the app; purchases are restored after signing in.

## When to escalate
If the customer still cannot sign in after these steps, escalate to Tech support with the device model, OS version and app version.
