# Duplicate or unexpected charges
## Pending authorisations
A temporary authorisation hold can look like a second charge. Holds disappear within 3–5 business days and are not real charges.

## Real duplicate charges
If two identical completed charges appear for the same period, the duplicate is a billing error. The agent should confirm both transaction IDs in the admin panel and refund the duplicate in full. Duplicate-charge refunds do not count against the $100 agent approval limit.

## Charged after cancelling
If the customer was charged after a confirmed cancellation, refund the charge and attach the cancellation confirmation to the ticket.
