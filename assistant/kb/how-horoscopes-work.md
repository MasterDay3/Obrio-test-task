# How horoscopes and reports are generated
## Personalisation
The daily horoscope is based on the full birth chart (date, time and place of birth), not only on the sun sign. Without birth time, it falls back to a sun-sign horoscope, which is less personal.

## Personal readings
Paid personal readings are written for the customer's chart. If two customers received identical paid readings, it is a content error and should be escalated to the Content team; the customer is eligible for a refund of that reading.
